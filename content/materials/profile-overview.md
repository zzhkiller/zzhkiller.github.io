# Zhouxing Zhao - Public Profile Overview

Zhouxing Zhao is an R&D Director, Chief Architect and Senior Engineer based in Hangzhou, China. His work focuses on AI engineering, multimodal systems, embodied intelligence, data governance, low-altitude UAV applications, cloud-edge collaboration, heterogeneous computing and intelligent scheduling systems.

He currently works at Zhejiang Communications Services Co., Ltd. His public profile combines enterprise architecture, engineering management, research-oriented product development, and digital transformation delivery.

Research and engineering interests include computer vision, large model applications, data governance, cloud-edge-device collaboration, low-altitude economy scenarios, UAV scheduling, DevOps platforms, knowledge graphs, and heterogeneous computing resource scheduling.

His [CNKI author profile](https://au.cnki.net/author/personalInfo/000076112695) lists research in computer software, multimodal systems, data elements and embodied intelligence. Earlier work includes activity-based costing, predictive control and data warehouses for pharmaceutical enterprises.

Since August 2026, he has served as a Committee Member of the Zhejiang Association for Artificial Intelligence. He is also a member of the Chinese Institute of Electronics (CIE) and the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI).
